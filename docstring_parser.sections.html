docstring_parser.sections

module documentation
(source)

Declare the sections, or fields, that a docstring style recognises.

A SectionSpec says which titles start a section, what its items document and how they are laid out. The same spec works for every style: in Google and Numpydoc its titles are section titles, and in ReST and Epydoc they are field names. Pass a list of specs as sections to docstring_parser.parse or to a style's parser to replace the style's own, which default_sections returns.

For example, to read Inputs: as a Google params section, and to keep a Todo: section as text of its own:

from docstring_parser import DocstringStyle, parse
from docstring_parser.sections import Kind, SectionSpec, default_sections

sections = [
    *default_sections(DocstringStyle.GOOGLE),
    SectionSpec("Inputs", Kind.PARAM),
    SectionSpec("Todo", Kind.TEXT),
]
parse(text, DocstringStyle.GOOGLE, sections=sections)
