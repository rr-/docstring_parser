class Problem(T.NamedTuple): (source)

View In Hierarchy

Something in a docstring that could not be parsed.

Its code names what is wrong, and stays the same from one version to the next, while the message may change:

bad-field
A ReST or Epydoc field that cannot be read, such as :param x with no colon after its head, or :: text with no name.
wrong-arguments
A field with too many or too few words in its head, such as :param a b c d: or :returns int str:.
mixed-returns-and-yields
An Epydoc docstring with both @return and @yield fields.
missing-colon
A Google item with no colon between its head and its description, in a section that takes several items, such as Args:.
missing-name
A Google section of params that holds text and no named item.
no-items
A Google section whose items cannot be told apart by their indent.
