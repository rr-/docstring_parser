class ParserCache(T.Generic[_P]): (source)

Constructor: ParserCache(size)

View In Hierarchy

Parsers or tables built for lists of sections, found by the list.

Building a parser compiles its title regex, so a parser is reused for an equal list of sections. Lists are compared rather than hashed: for the same section objects, which is how sections are passed again and again, the comparison stops at their identity.
